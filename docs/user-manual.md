# User Manual

*(Documentation member: add screenshots as the mobile screens are finished.)*

1. **Create an account:** open the app, tap Register, fill in username, email, and password.
2. **Log in:** enter your username and password.
3. **Create a wallet:** for example Cash or GCash, with your starting balance.
4. **Add income or an expense:** pick a wallet, a category, the amount, and the date.
5. **View history:** open Transactions to see all records.
6. **Set a budget:** choose an expense category, an amount, and a date range.
7. **Create a savings goal:** set a name, target amount, and deadline.
8. **Dashboard:** shows your total income, total expenses, and balance.
